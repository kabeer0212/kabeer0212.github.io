Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how My Storage App (“the app”) accesses, uses, stores and shares information, including data from your Google Account. The app is a small personal project run by its owner (“I”, “me”). It runs on the owner's own computer.

1. Information the app accesses

When you connect a Google account, you grant the app the permissions shown on Google's consent screen. Through them the app accesses:

  • Basic account information: the account's email address, name and profile picture, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Google Drive storage information: how much storage is used and available in the account.
  • Google Drive files: files you upload through the app, the details of those files (name, size, type, folder and ID), and the contents of a file only when you ask to preview or download it.
  • Sign-in tokens: access and refresh tokens that Google issues so the app can keep working for the account you connected.

The app also keeps the email address and password hash for the app's own login, if you create one.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to provide the features you see in the app: connecting accounts, showing storage, uploading files to an account with enough free space, listing, previewing, downloading, organizing and deleting your files. It is not used for advertising, profiling or marketing, and it is not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

3. How the information is stored

  • Files you upload are streamed directly to your Google Drive. The app does not keep copies of your files on its server.
  • The app keeps a list of file details and the Google sign-in tokens in a database that runs on the owner's own computer. The Google tokens are encrypted in that database.

4. Sharing

I do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Data goes only between the app and Google, as needed to do what you ask. I may disclose information if the law requires it.

Google API Services User Data Policy. The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Keeping and deleting your information

  • You can disconnect a Google account from the app's Settings page at any time.
  • You can also remove the app's access to your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To ask me to delete the information stored about you, email kabeergour21@gmail.com.
  • Files you uploaded stay in your own Google Drive until you delete them there or in the app.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update the date at the top of this page.

8. Contact
